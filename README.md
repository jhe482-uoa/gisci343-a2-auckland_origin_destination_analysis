## 1. Motivation and Audience

### 1.1 What problem does your dashboard address?

<!-- State the specific question your dashboard helps someone answer. -->
<!-- Example: "How has public transport patronage in Auckland recovered since 2020?" -->

The key problem that this dashboard answers is "Are residents being pushed further away from the economic or education hub over time?" - this is a key indicator of the housing market as to whether affordable housing is supplied near the high-demand employment/education hub. The key area of focus is SA2s in Auckland, as Auckland is NZ's biggest city. Census data from 2023 and 2018 is used and weighted average travel distance is applied to answer this question. Origin-destination pairs is analysed. The user can easily visualise the catchment of any given SA2s.

### 1.2 Who is it for?

<!-- Describe one or two realistic users — their role and the decision this dashboard informs. -->

Two possible user for this dashboard could be property developers or council urban planners. Their goal is similar but their role and contribution is different. Property developer can identify which SA2 is a popular destination yet the average travel distance is high. They can use this information for decide where their next residential development should be, as people prefer to live close to their destination, implying high demand for housing in that area. Whereas council planner can upzone certain area to allow high density residential development. To address high average travel distance.

Comparison of weighted average travel distance for work/study between 2023 & 2018.
